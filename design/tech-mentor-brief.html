HyroxCoach · Technical brief

Ten days, six questions

I spent the Act phase of my challenge measuring watchOS behaviour rather than building features. Before I design the interface, I want to know whether my structural choices are sound — or whether I've built something that works for the wrong reasons.

What I built

A watchOS app that records a HYROX-structured workout, and an iOS app that reviews it afterwards. The watch owns the live session; the phone owns everything after it.

HKWorkoutSession and HKLiveWorkoutBuilder on the watch. A state machine over run → roxzone → station. Segment boundaries persisted atomically to disk on every transition. A semantic summary attached as HKWorkout metadata at finishWorkout.

No transport code of my own. The data reaches the phone on HealthKit's own sync.

What I decided

Not to build WatchConnectivity at all. Every capability it was planned to provide had been removed by measurement: the workout already crosses, metadata carries the station data, a full race fits, and both records share one anchor so nothing needs merging.

The only thing metadata cannot carry is live data during the workout — and the iOS app does not display live data, by design. I wrote a decision record with reversal conditions rather than building it.

This is the decision I would most like a second opinion on.

Six questions

In priority order. The first is the one I am least sure about.

1
Structural

Should segments be HKWorkoutEvent, not metadata?

I encoded stations as a custom metadata string — name@offsetSeconds, joined and attached at finish. HealthKit has HKWorkoutEvent with .segment and .marker types, and I did not use it.

My approach works and is measured. But is metadata-as-carrier a legitimate pattern here, or am I using a field that happens to work for something it was not meant for? Would .segment events survive OS changes better, or appear in Apple's own Health UI?

2
Durability

Does deleting the app remove its HealthKit samples?

My whole architecture assumes HealthKit keeps the record. I have never tested it. If an app's samples die with the app, an athlete's entire history depends on never reinstalling — which would change the decision above.

3
Limits

What is the metadata size limit?

Undocumented as far as I can find. 601 characters passed comfortably and I never found the ceiling. I expect a limit would truncate silently rather than raise an error — is that right?

4
Modelling

What do real apps do about activityType?

HYROX has no HealthKit type, so every workout is permanently .crossTraining — a slightly wrong description, forever. What do you use, and does the choice affect rings, categorisation or energy estimation?

5
Interaction

Correction on the watch, mid-effort

I designed an undo that restores a mis-tapped segment's original timestamps. It works. I did not use it when I actually mis-tapped during a real session, because it lives on a different screen. What works for correction on a watch, out of breath, one-handed?

6
Scale

What breaks at 90 minutes that does not at 5?

My longest real session was 5 minutes 11 seconds. Battery, session longevity, memory pressure and background behaviour across a full race are all untested.

The honest summary

I have numbers for how the platform behaves and a workbook of what I predicted against what happened. Three of my cycles ended by proving planned work unnecessary, so I built less than I intended, on purpose.

What I do not have is any way to know whether the structure underneath is the one an experienced person would have chosen.
